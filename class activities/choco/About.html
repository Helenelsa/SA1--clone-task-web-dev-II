Our Story

History of Westons Chocolate Box

Since 1950, Westons Chocolate Box has been delighting chocolate lovers with our artisan creations. What started as a small family business has grown into a renowned chocolate brand known for its luxury offerings, exquisite flavors, and uncompromising quality. Our mission is to create unforgettable chocolate experiences that bring joy to every occasion.

Meet Our Chocolatiers

James Weston

James is the grandson of our founder and a master chocolatier who carries on the family legacy. His passion for creating innovative flavors and perfecting traditional recipes ensures every chocolate is made with love and expertise.

Sophia Davis

Sophia is our head chocolatier with over 20 years of experience. Her creativity and attention to detail make her a key part of our team. Sophia believes that each chocolate should be a work of art, both visually and in flavor.

Liam Cooper

With a background in pastry and confectionery, Liam adds a gourmet touch to our chocolate creations. His expertise in textures and ingredients ensures that every bite is a culinary masterpiece.

Our Chocolate-Making Process

1. Sourcing the Finest Ingredients

We carefully select the highest quality cacao beans from sustainable farms, ensuring ethical and eco-friendly practices are followed.

2. Crafting by Hand

Every chocolate is handcrafted by our team of expert chocolatiers using time-honored techniques and innovative methods.

3. Packaging with Care

We believe that presentation matters. Our chocolates are carefully packaged in eco-friendly, elegant boxes perfect for gifting.
